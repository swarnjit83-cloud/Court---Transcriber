# Privacy Policy – court-video-bot

court-video-bot is a personal tool that creates videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- It asks only for permission to upload videos to the account of the person who signs in (the owner).
- It does not collect, sell, share or store personal information about any other person.
- The sign-in token is kept privately by the owner (in the owner's own GitHub repository secrets) and is used only to upload videos.
- The app is not offered to the public. No data is shared with third parties.
