Help & how this works ▾

What this is: a single web page (no app-store install). It uses AssemblyAI's speech-to-text service to do the actual transcription — your audio is uploaded directly from this page to AssemblyAI using the API key you paste in above. Nothing passes through any other server.

Cost: AssemblyAI is pay-as-you-go (their free tier includes a large number of free hours to start, no card needed). Check assemblyai.com/pricing for current rates — this page doesn't mark anything up, you're billed directly by AssemblyAI.

Multi-hour recordings: AssemblyAI's transcription service accepts audio up to about 10 hours long in one go, so a long hearing or dictation is fine as a single upload. The practical limit on this end is your phone's upload speed and whether the in-app recorder stays running — see the note above about using your phone's own voice-recorder app for anything long.

Noise cleanup: when you record directly in this app, the audio is passed through a simple filter (removes low rumble/hum, evens out volume) in real time before saving. This is a basic cleanup, not full noise removal — it won't erase loud background conversation, for example. It does not apply to files you upload from elsewhere; AssemblyAI's own model is reasonably tolerant of background noise on its own.

Accuracy first: with "exact wording" on, the transcript keeps filler words exactly as said rather than a machine-cleaned rewrite. Everything is editable — tap into the transcript or summary boxes above and correct anything by hand before exporting.

If something errors out: a "network error" right after you start a transcription usually means AssemblyAI is not accepting requests directly from a browser for your account — that would need a small relay step added later; the error banner will say so.
